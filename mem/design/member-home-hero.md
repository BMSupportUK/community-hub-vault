---
name: Member home hero spacing
description: Welcome copy is large and the subscription box closes the space beneath it — no big empty area
type: preference
---

On the member (non-staff) home hero, the welcome copy must read large — heading at
text-2xl/3xl, the two supporting lines at text-base/18px and text-base/16px with
generous line spacing — and it owns the column's spare height so the greeting,
heading and lines are evenly spaced rather than bunched at the top.

The subscription box sits beneath that copy and stretches to absorb whatever column
height is left, so no large empty area is visible below it. It stops growing at a
modest cap so a very tall window cannot stretch it hollow, and on phones it keeps its
original compact sizing and padding, where the enlarged treatment would clip the
account details.

Never solve leftover space by enlarging the hero image on desktop: the photo is 16:9
and a tall crop loses the people in it.
