# Two new cartoon themes: Stewie and Bart Simpson

## What you'll get
- **Stewie**: a theme based on Stewie Griffin from Family Guy. He's in his red dungarees and yellow top, standing with a toy ray gun in the family living room, with a bright cartoon backdrop.
- **Bart Simpson**: a theme based on Bart from The Simpsons. He's on his skateboard in front of the Simpsons' house in a sunny cartoon Springfield street.
- Both go in the existing **Cartoons** group, next to Dopey, and both are switched on.
- Each picture keeps its middle lighter so the home-screen tiles stay easy to read, like the other themes.
- Fox owns these characters, so the pictures will look a lot like them. That's fine for your own use, but I'll leave out the show logos and title text.

## How it goes live
1. Create both pictures and size them for the TV screen.
2. Publish the website, then wait until both pictures load from bmsupport.uk.
3. Only then add them to the theme list, so nobody ever gets a blank background.
4. I'll also check the Jolly Boys' Outing picture and switch that theme back on once it's live.
5. Boxes, Windows and Mac pick them up the next time they check, with no app update needed.

## Technical details
- Files: `public/player-themes/stewie-v1.jpg` and `bart-v1.jpg`, 1920x1080, quality 84.
- New `player_themes` rows in grp "Cartoons": Stewie (sort 71, accent #DC2626) and Bart Simpson (sort 72, accent #FACC15), enabled once the pictures load with a 200 from the live site.
