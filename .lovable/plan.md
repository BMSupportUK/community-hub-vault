# Downloader app installation video

## Final video
- Produce one polished MP4 tutorial, approximately 2 minutes long, in 1920×1080 landscape format.
- Recreate the Amazon Fire TV screens rather than using a live screen recording.
- Use a friendly British female voice for the supplied narration.
- Keep all important steps understandable without sound through concise on-screen labels and highlighted controls.

## Storyboard
1. **Hook and introduction — 0:00–0:15**
   - Open on a recreated Fire TV home screen.
   - Zoom toward **Find** and highlight it with a simple animated pointer.
   - Introduce the Downloader app and its purpose.

2. **Search and download — 0:15–0:45**
   - Show **Find → Search**, typing “Downloader,” selecting the orange AFTVnews app tile, and choosing **Get / Download**.
   - Clearly identify the official orange app tile and developer name.

3. **Enable Developer Options — 0:45–1:30**
   - Show **Settings → My Fire TV → About**.
   - Animate seven presses on the device name, including a visible count and the unlocked confirmation.
   - Return to **Developer Options → Install unknown apps** and switch Downloader to **ON**.

4. **First run and test — 1:30–2:00**
   - Open Downloader and show the storage permission prompt.
   - Select **Allow**, then focus attention on the URL/code entry box.
   - End on a clean completion frame confirming that Downloader is ready.

## Visual and motion direction
- Use a faithful Fire TV-style dark interface with orange Downloader branding, large readable labels, and TV-safe spacing.
- Use smooth camera pushes, focus highlights, pointer arrows, and short button-press animations instead of rapid cuts.
- Keep recreated screens clearly presented as tutorial visuals without inserting real accounts, personal details, or URLs.

## Audio and delivery
- Record the supplied script with a clear, friendly British female voice and synchronize each sentence to its matching action.
- Mix narration cleanly and keep any supporting sound effects subtle.
- Check key frames for readable text, verify the complete MP4 and audio track, then save the finished video to Files.

## Technical details
- Build the screen recreations and animation as a frame-based Remotion video at 30 fps.
- Use deterministic timing for every cursor movement, zoom, highlight, screen change, and caption.
- Render to H.264 MP4 and verify its resolution, duration, file size, and audio before delivery.
