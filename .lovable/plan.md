# Organise completed incidents by date

## Changes
- Keep active incidents together under **Active Issues**.
- Under **Completed**, add year tabs based on each incident’s resolved date, newest year first.
- Add January–December tabs within the selected year, showing counts only for months containing incidents.
- Place each completed incident card under its matching resolved year and month, defaulting to the newest available period.
- Keep incident details, updates, editing and notifications unchanged.
