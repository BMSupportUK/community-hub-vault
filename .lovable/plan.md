- Add clear **Account setup** and **Sales** categories to the Automated messages & emails page.
- Move sign-up, invitation, login, password, verification, approval, rejection/ban, and account-security emails into Account setup.

- Make the category controls explicit and stable so neither category disappears because of record ordering.
- Keep all current HTML editing, image upload, placeholders, subjects, and admin-only access unchanged.
