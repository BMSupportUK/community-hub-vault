# Non-blocking ticket notice in Talk Channels

## Goal
Keep staff controls usable when outstanding tickets are detected in Talk Channels.

## Change
- On Talk Channel pages, show the outstanding-ticket alert as a small corner notice instead of a blocking popup.
- Keep the ticket count and a direct action to open the ticket list.
- Leave the existing popup unchanged everywhere else.
- Keep live new-ticket notices unchanged.

## Verification
- Open a Talk Channel with outstanding tickets and confirm the page controls remain clickable.
- Confirm the notice opens the ticket list.
- Confirm the normal popup still appears outside Talk Channels.
