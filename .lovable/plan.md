# BM Support App Store installation videos

## Deliverables
- Create two separate Full HD MP4 guides at 1920×1080 and 30 fps:
  1. **Install BM Support App Store on Android TV**
  2. **Install BM Support App Store on Amazon Fire TV**
- Use friendly British female narration, readable captions, television-safe spacing, and recreated device screens.
- Save both finished videos to Files for review. Do not replace any live install-guide video.

## Android TV guide
1. Open the Google Play Store, search for **Downloader by AFTVnews**, and install it.
2. Open Downloader and accept the required permission prompts.
3. If prompted, open Android TV settings and allow Downloader under **Install unknown apps**.
4. Return to Downloader and enter the clearly labelled sample address **bmsupport.uk/1234567**.
5. Download the BM Support App Store APK, choose **Install**, then **Open**.
6. Finish on the BM Support App Store ready screen, with a reminder to use the personal 24-hour address shown in BM Support.

## Amazon Fire TV guide
1. From the Fire TV home screen, open **Find → Search** and install **Downloader by AFTVnews**.
2. Open **Settings → My Fire TV → About** and press the device name seven times if Developer Options is hidden.
3. Open **Developer Options → Install unknown apps** and switch Downloader **ON**.
4. Open Downloader, accept its permission prompt, and enter the labelled sample address **bmsupport.uk/1234567**.
5. Download the BM Support App Store APK, choose **Install**, then **Open**.
6. Finish on the BM Support App Store ready screen, with a reminder to use the member's own 24-hour address.
