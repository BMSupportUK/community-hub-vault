# tv-player activity rules
- Live TV (Android/Fire TV) is the Sky-style guide itself (showTvGuide(cat, live=true)): preview box on top, GuideGridView below, categories in the slide-out side panel; OK on an on-now programme previews it, OK again opens the channel menu; the home screen has no separate TV guide tile; a left-edge tab like the website order-chat side button (accent, vertically centred, ☰ icon over a vertical "Channels" label) opens the category panel (no category button in the Now/Day row); on phones the preview sits alone with no guide grid beneath it; the "View EPG" pill in the header row before Search TV opens the guide full screen, and the Now/Day controls live in that pop-up's bar (the normal view drops that row); the guide is a Sky-style grid (GuideGridView: channels × 2h time window (3h on phones), lazy cached EPG, 3 parallel fetches) and is the only place reminders are set.
- The TV guide's category list is a side panel (sideDrawer/showPanel/hidePanel) that slides in from the left edge, never a pop-up at the top; LiveChannelDrawer slides from categories to their channel list without closing the panel, and channel selection aligns the grid and starts its preview. Back or the scrim closes the drawer first, and the screen behind it is blocked from focus while it is open.
- GuideGridView handles vertical (rows) and horizontal (30-min time steps, never before now) touch drags itself (no outer ScrollView around the grid), suppresses selection after swiping, and retains remote row/page navigation so the preview remains above the scrolling guide.
- Phone guide screens apply GuideSafeArea system/gesture/cutout insets to their outer frame; never disable Android navigation, and cancelled guide touches cannot select a channel.
- Catch up: channels with provider `tv_archive` show a clock in the TV guide channel column (Left at "now" only highlights the clock and OK opens it; tap on phones); it opens a day → programme list from `get_simple_data_table` and plays Xtream `/timeshift/` URLs (UK time) through VLC on Amazon devices only (other Android uses the built-in player, CatchupFallback.useVlc), falling back to the built-in player when VLC is missing; catch-up plays never go into Recently watched. Catch-up VLC launches add `disable_hardware`; a VLC error result (or a return under 10s with no position, CatchupFallback) replays the recording in the built-in player. Built-in catch up waits for full keyframes and prefers software decoders (hardware ones smear timeshift into blocks), falling back to hardware.
- Manual EPG time shift stores the device UTC offset; a clock change resets it to 0 so the guide is never an hour out.
- Android films/episodes ask nothing up front: the built-in player turns on existing subtitles and only then asks whether to turn them off (VLC is not asked). Windows and Mac do the same: engines report subtitle track counts and the main process pauses to ask only when tracks exist.
- Favourites and Recently watched open on Live TV / Movies / Series tabs (episodes count as Series) on Android, Windows and Mac; sports guide event rows keep a blank line between events.
- Search and voice results use resultList(): a ListView dialog with a border-only selector, one row per D-pad press, OK opens the row.
- Series seasons use bindSeriesSeasons: focus never reloads episodes, Down enters the chosen season's non-focusable episode rows, Up returns to its pill, and OK activates the selected episode; selection rings draw above rows.
- Favourites sync through the device-token `favourites`/`favourites_set` actions: one whole list per account owner and provider login (last write wins); pulled on unlock and when Favourites opens, pushed on every change; a never-stored account uploads the device's existing list, and each device's first sync merges its own favourites into the account list instead of being overwritten.
- Admin/management announcements are one-off: the server ends a row 3 minutes after sending; the newest active player_announcements row is served by the device-token `announcement` action (10s shared cache); every app checks it every 15s and draws a closable bottom bar over every screen only if the app was already open when it was sent (server age_ms vs app start) (Android adds it to the window decor; desktop shrinks screens so video never covers it); a closed announcement id is remembered per device. Android keeps the bar clear of system-bar insets, closes it on a bar tap and catches Back in dispatchKeyEvent before the screen's own Back.
- Catch-up playback builds its own player without non-IDR keyframe starts so mid-GOP timeshift recordings don't decode as blocks; live keeps the faster start.
- Major announcements are a separate player_major_announcements list (admin tab of its own), served by the device-token `major_announcements` action (30s shared cache); apps poll every 60s and show a top-right icon with an unread counter (seen ids stored per device); Android draws it only on MainActivity, never over full-screen video; a signed-out app shows no icon and keeps no list (Windows/Mac gate on the unlocked state).
- Every text-entry box stays clear of the on-screen keypad: number-only full screens (account number) never open the system keyboard and type through the in-app NumberPad instead, because a sideways phone leaves no room above a system keyboard; other dialogs go through keepEntryVisible() (top-anchored, ADJUST_PAN, no extract UI, shrunk to the space above the measured IME inset).
- Sound booster: a per-device level (off/low/medium/high) drives a LoudnessEnhancer attached in PlayerActivity.build() to every built-in player's audio session (SoundBoost.java); external VLC is untouched.
- Anti-freeze: picture (Android frame count, desktop play position) unchanged 4s while playing reopens the stream (live at now), live >30s behind jumps to now, and 2 stalls remember the other ts/m3u8 format per channel. Anti-buffering numbers live in StreamRecovery (Android) and windows-bm-player/stream-recovery.cjs (Windows/Mac) — keep them in step: default "smooth" buffer, reconnect after a 12s stall or a drop after first play with 1/2/4/8/10s back-off (8 tries, VOD resumes at position), 2 stalls in 2 min cap bitrate (Android maxVideoBitrate, VLC adaptive-maxheight), 3 on an HD-named channel offer the SD sibling. Stalls batch to the device-token `buffering` action (player_buffer_events, admin/management read via player_buffer_summary); 5 devices on one live channel in 10 min raise one hourly staff notification.
- A Live TV preview that reaches playing reports itself as Live TV viewing (np_* now-playing, np_preview flag) until it stops, so it shows on Live connections; the website never credits previews toward viewing hours (watching_preview skips the watch credit).
- Live TV previews never start on highlight (Android: the guide grid's highlight never previews in live mode): the preview box shows the family-on-sofa picture (father pointing a remote at the TV, no text in the picture) until a channel is clicked/OK'd; the instruction line sits under the box (phones title the info "Current programme", show the chosen category under it and put a "▶ Preview channel" button beneath those details); stopPreview shows the picture again. OK again on the previewing channel opens its menu; on phones a single tap on a playing preview opens that menu and the button becomes "▶ Play menu" (previewStarted/previewStopped keep the label in step).
- Guide line-ups go through GuideListings: short EPG first, the full day table when it only reaches "now"; cached per channel for 15 minutes or until it runs out, and the grid re-asks every minute so programmes move on without reopening Live TV.
- Phone play menus (open()) add "📺 Cast to TV": DlnaCast does SSDP AVTransport discovery under a Wi-Fi multicast lock, then SetAVTransportURI + Play on the chosen smart TV; TVs and Android TV boxes do not show the option.
- Report a problem lives on the built-in player screen (top-left button shown with the controls, ADJUST_PAN pop-up, no keepEntryVisible resizing which flickered phones); play menus never carry it — the series page keeps its own button.
