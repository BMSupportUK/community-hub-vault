# tv-player activity rules
- Upcoming programme selections and their reminder icons confirm reminder changes before preview routing; current programmes/channel labels retain normal playback behaviour.
- Channel/preview EPG shares FixtureGuide: non-official sports/club feeds first read saved website fixtures, authoritative responses bypass provider expansion, ordinary/unmatched channels use provider short/full-day tables. Category entry never calls country-refresh endpoints.
- Phone Live TV uses its dedicated View EPG screen mode while TV boxes keep the always-visible grid, so the guide has usable space on short phone screens; LiveGuideHeaderTest covers both modes.
- Live TV on phones was the Sky-style guide screen (showTvGuide(cat, live=true)); TV boxes (Android/Google TV/Fire TV) use the same guide screen with the grid always visible: preview top-left, programme info beside it (title, times, progress/minutes left, description, Next line, favourite star, category) with Search TV/Voice search pills top-right, date above the grid. Box top row is non-baseline-aligned (baseline alignment scatters the info text) and the info panel is a stack of single-line or line-capped rows with minutes left inside the time text (side-by-side text views squeeze and stretch the panel), preview ~30% width capped at 32% height, and a slim date row carries compact miniPill ◀ 24h / Now / 24h ▶ controls. Guide screen details: preview box on top, GuideGridView below, categories in the slide-out side panel; OK on an on-now programme previews it, OK again opens the channel menu; the home screen has no separate TV guide tile; there is no persistent left-edge Categories tab on phones or boxes — TV boxes get a bold "◀ Press Left on your remote to list the categories" hint beside the Category pill after the day jumps, and phones keep the Categories pill in the View EPG pop-up bar; on phones the preview sits alone with no guide grid beneath it; the "View EPG" pill in the header row before Search TV opens the guide full screen, and the Now/Day controls live in that pop-up's bar (the normal view drops that row); the guide is a Sky-style grid (GuideGridView: channels × 2h time window (3h on phones), lazy cached EPG, 3 parallel fetches) and is the only place reminders are set.
- Full-screen live playback on TV/Fire TV boxes: Left with the controls hidden slides the guide's category/channel drawer over the playing picture (LiveDrawerData snapshot taken when the TV guide loads); the picture keeps playing underneath, Back or Left closes it, and picking a channel sets LiveDrawerData.pendingPick and finishes so the main screen hands the old stream back to the guide preview and opens the new channel through the normal playback path. Films, episodes, catch up and phones are unchanged.
- The TV guide's category list is a side panel (sideDrawer/showPanel/hidePanel) that slides in from the left edge, never a pop-up at the top; no persistent left-edge Categories tab is drawn. On TV/Fire TV boxes Left from any guide row/time opens it; the Category pill is display-only. Closing restores grid focus without changing row/time/scroll; selecting the same category preserves position, while a different category starts at its first channel. The drawer's scoped return-focus target restores focus immediately and after its closing animation so Android cannot send it to Search TV. On phones LiveChannelDrawer slides from categories to their channel list without closing the panel, and channel selection aligns the grid and starts its preview. Back or the scrim closes the drawer first, and the screen behind it is blocked from focus while it is open. Phone Left/time/catch-up navigation remains unchanged.
- GuideGridView handles vertical (rows) and horizontal (30-min time steps, never before now) touch drags itself (no outer ScrollView around the grid), suppresses selection after swiping, and retains remote row/page navigation so the preview remains above the scrolling guide.
- Phone guide screens apply GuideSafeArea system/gesture/cutout insets to their outer frame; never disable Android navigation, and cancelled guide touches cannot select a channel.
- Catch up: channels with provider `tv_archive` show a clock in the TV guide channel column (Left at "now" only highlights the clock and OK opens it; tap on phones); it opens a day → programme list from `get_simple_data_table` and plays Xtream `/timeshift/` URLs (UK time) through the built-in player on every device (VLC was removed from the app); catch-up plays never go into Recently watched. Built-in catch up waits for full keyframes and prefers software decoders (hardware ones smear timeshift into blocks), falling back to hardware.
- Android films/episodes ask nothing up front: the built-in player turns on existing subtitles and only then asks whether to turn them off; both engines also show a 💬 Subtitles button (PlayerActionControls) to switch them off or pick another track at any time. Windows and Mac do the same: engines report subtitle track counts and the main process pauses to ask only when tracks exist.
- Series seasons use bindSeriesSeasons: focus never reloads episodes, Down enters the chosen season's non-focusable episode rows, Up returns to its pill, and OK activates the selected episode; selection rings draw above rows.
- Catch-up playback builds its own player without non-IDR keyframe starts so mid-GOP timeshift recordings don't decode as blocks; live keeps the faster start.
- A Live TV preview that reaches playing reports itself as Live TV viewing (np_* now-playing, np_preview flag) until it stops, so it shows on Live connections; the website never credits previews toward viewing hours (watching_preview skips the watch credit).
- Live TV previews never start on highlight (Android: the guide grid's highlight never previews in live mode): the preview box shows the family-on-sofa picture (father pointing a remote at the TV, no text in the picture) until a channel is clicked/OK'd; the instruction line sits under the box (phones title the info "Current programme", show the chosen category under it and put a "▶ Preview channel" button beneath those details); stopPreview shows the picture again. OK again on the previewing channel opens its menu; on phones a single tap on a playing preview opens that menu and the button becomes "▶ Play menu" (previewStarted/previewStopped keep the label in step).
- Report a problem lives on the built-in player screen (bottom-left button beside Sound booster, shown with the controls, ADJUST_PAN pop-up, no keepEntryVisible resizing which flickered phones); play menus never carry it — the series page keeps its own button. The app posts the `report` action (names, category, episode, device, version, customer text) and the website opens a Live TV or Movie/Series ticket for the linked owner.
- Playback-engine rules (anti-freeze and live cushion, favourites sync, sound booster, guide listings, DLNA cast, backup hand-over, player controls, EPG time shift, swap channel, shared-login takeover) live in `tv-player/app/src/main/java/uk/bmsupport/AGENTS.md`.
- Search/voice, keypad, tab and Films/Series layout rules live in `tv-player/app/AGENTS.md`.
- Announcement rules (admin/management and major announcements) live in `tv-player/app/src/main/AGENTS.md`.
- TV boxes draw the live preview on one window-decor SurfaceView "stage" over the preview box; opening full screen from it grows the stage to the screen under the see-through PlayerStageActivity (PlayerView with no surface) and Back hands the player back and shrinks it, because changing surface restarts many boxes' decoders (black). Phones and non-carried opens keep PreviewHandoff + PreviewSnapshot into the normal PlayerActivity.
- Sports Guide previews a channel in its own preview box first (OK previews, OK again on the same channel carries it full screen), reusing the Live TV stage/hand-back so neither direction reconnects.
- The home-screen 50Hz button also resets video: it releases the preview and any carried/hand-back players and drops the stage surface before the display mode changes.
- The guide-load drawer snapshot must stay cheap: never call streamUrls() (Keystore binder) or categoryName() (reads cache file) per channel; use the loaded category rows. Both caused ANRs/slow EPG loads.
