# tv-player playback and sync rules
- Manual EPG time shift stores the device UTC offset; a clock change resets it to 0 so the guide is never an hour out.
- Favourites sync through the device-token `favourites`/`favourites_set` actions: one whole list per account owner and provider login (last write wins); pulled on unlock and when Favourites opens, pushed on every change; a never-stored account uploads the device's existing list, and each device's first sync merges its own favourites into the account list instead of being overwritten.
- Sound booster: a per-device level (off/low/medium/high) drives a LoudnessEnhancer attached in PlayerActivity.build() to every built-in player's audio session (SoundBoost.java); external VLC is untouched.
- Anti-freeze: picture (Android frame count, desktop play position) unchanged 4s while playing reopens the stream (live at now), live >30s behind jumps to now. Anti-buffering numbers live in StreamRecovery (Android) and windows-bm-player/stream-recovery.cjs (Windows/Mac) — keep them in step: default "smooth" buffer, reconnect after a 12s stall or a drop after first play with 1/2/4/8/10s back-off (8 tries, VOD resumes at position), 2 stalls in 2 min cap bitrate (Android maxVideoBitrate, VLC adaptive-maxheight), 3 on an HD-named channel offer the SD sibling. Stalls batch to the device-token `buffering` action (player_buffer_events, admin/management read via player_buffer_summary); 5 devices on one live channel in 10 min raise one hourly staff notification.
- Android HLS live keeps the provider's own hold-back (no LiveConfiguration offset, only a 0.97–1.0 speed range so low buffers ease instead of stalling or racing the edge; segment loads retry 6 times in place; forced offsets parked playback at the oldest Xtream segment and flushed the buffer) and drift is measured from that live point; channels never switch or share ts/m3u8 because of buffering (playlist order only; next address only when one fails to open). Desktop keeps liveBufferMs. Capped quality returns after 2 stall-free minutes; the steadier ts/m3u8 per channel is shared across all devices via device-token `formats`/`format_set` (player_channel_formats), so one device's lesson helps everyone.
- Guide line-ups parse through GuideListings; FixtureGuide restores saved website sports/club fixtures before provider fallback, with visible-row refresh. Ordinary/official channels use provider tables; country guide imports remain disabled.
- Phone play menus (open()) add "📺 Cast to TV": DlnaCast does SSDP AVTransport discovery under a Wi-Fi multicast lock, then SetAVTransportURI + Play on the chosen smart TV; TVs and Android TV boxes do not show the option.
- Built-in playback hands over to BackupPlayerActivity (in-app libVLC, ARM-only native libs) when every address fails before a picture or reconnects give up; catch-up keeps its own recovery.
- Player controls per kind live in PlayerControls: fast forward/rewind only on films and episodes (not live or catch up); no Report a problem on catch up.
- Full-screen playback matches content frame rate at the same resolution with a 1.5s advance switch note. Android TV startup leaves IntroActivity at the device rate until the opening film ends, then shows the 50Hz note, waits 1.5s, requests 50Hz and settles before MainActivity's link screen; other activity windows pin 50Hz even when already matching to prevent default resets. Phones and Fire TV skip this startup step; Fire OS matches playback itself.
- Fresh full-screen opens keep the "Channel Loading" family picture cover and muted sound until the frame rate is known (remembered per channel to switch the TV while connecting), plus a settle delay after an actual mode switch, so the TV re-sync blank never interrupts a picture.
- Sports Guide crosses: PlayerActivity reports `channel_health` black (no video frame 10s after first ready) and ok (2 stall-free minutes); BackupPlayerActivity reports failed when both engines give up; desktop main.cjs healthTick mirrors this. Website rules live in src/lib/player-channel-health.ts.
- Swap channel (Android ChannelSwap.java, desktop channel-variants.cjs, keep in step): live-only list of same Sports Guide event feeds, then same-name quality/country versions, Not working ones last and unselectable; offered automatically after 3 stalls in 2 min on any live channel; swaps post swap rows (swapped_to/swap_reason) that diagnostics count apart from stalls.
- Shared-login takeover goes through StreamClaim.java (device-token `stream_claim` before preview/full playback, fail-open; `stop_stream` from the announcement reply finishes player activities and the preview).
- Advert/programme switches (sound or picture format change, stream clock jump, size change) start a 10s grace: the frozen-picture check is paused and a stall that began at a switch waits 10s longer before reconnecting, so the buffer is kept through ad breaks.
- Provider restarts (live stream ended) reopen at once on every channel, keep the last picture (setKeepContentOnPlayerReset) and show Reconnecting only after 3s; every feed switch type (picture/sound/timing/frame_rate/provider_restart) goes to the device-token `feed_switches` action (player_feed_switches, admin/management summary RPC), once per channel+type per 30s, Android and desktop alike.
