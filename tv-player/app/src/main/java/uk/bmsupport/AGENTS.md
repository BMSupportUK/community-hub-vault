# tv-player playback and sync rules
- Manual EPG time shift stores the device UTC offset; a clock change resets it to 0 so the guide is never an hour out.
- Favourites sync through the device-token `favourites`/`favourites_set` actions: one whole list per account owner and provider login (last write wins); pulled on unlock and when Favourites opens, pushed on every change; a never-stored account uploads the device's existing list, and each device's first sync merges its own favourites into the account list instead of being overwritten.
- Sound booster: a per-device level (off/low/medium/high) drives a LoudnessEnhancer attached in PlayerActivity.build() to every built-in player's audio session (SoundBoost.java); external VLC is untouched.
- Anti-freeze: picture (Android frame count, desktop play position) unchanged 4s while playing reopens the stream (live at now), live >30s behind jumps to now, and 2 stalls remember the other ts/m3u8 format per channel. Anti-buffering numbers live in StreamRecovery (Android) and windows-bm-player/stream-recovery.cjs (Windows/Mac) — keep them in step: default "smooth" buffer, reconnect after a 12s stall or a drop after first play with 1/2/4/8/10s back-off (8 tries, VOD resumes at position), 2 stalls in 2 min cap bitrate (Android maxVideoBitrate, VLC adaptive-maxheight), 3 on an HD-named channel offer the SD sibling. Stalls batch to the device-token `buffering` action (player_buffer_events, admin/management read via player_buffer_summary); 5 devices on one live channel in 10 min raise one hourly staff notification.
- Live playback keeps a ~12s cushion behind the edge (Android LiveConfiguration, desktop liveBufferMs) and capped quality returns after 2 stall-free minutes; the steadier ts/m3u8 per channel is shared across all devices via device-token `formats`/`format_set` (player_channel_formats), so one device's lesson helps everyone.
- Guide line-ups go through GuideListings: short EPG first, the full day table when it only reaches "now"; cached per channel for 15 minutes or until it runs out, and the grid re-asks every minute so programmes move on without reopening Live TV.
- Phone play menus (open()) add "📺 Cast to TV": DlnaCast does SSDP AVTransport discovery under a Wi-Fi multicast lock, then SetAVTransportURI + Play on the chosen smart TV; TVs and Android TV boxes do not show the option.
- Built-in playback hands over to BackupPlayerActivity (in-app libVLC, ARM-only native libs) when every address fails before a picture or reconnects give up; catch-up keeps its own recovery.
- Player controls per kind live in PlayerControls: fast forward/rewind only on films and episodes (not live or catch up); no Report a problem on catch up.
- Full-screen playback asks Android boxes/phones for a display mode whose refresh rate is a whole multiple of the content frame rate (FrameRateMatch; measured from frame times when TS gives none), same resolution only; Fire TV is skipped because Fire OS matches itself.
- Sports Guide crosses: PlayerActivity reports `channel_health` black (no video frame 10s after first ready) and ok (2 stall-free minutes); BackupPlayerActivity reports failed when both engines give up; desktop main.cjs healthTick mirrors this. Website rules live in src/lib/player-channel-health.ts.
