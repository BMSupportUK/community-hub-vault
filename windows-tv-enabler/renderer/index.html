BM TV Enabler

Fire TV / Android TV remote-control setup

Welcome to BM TV Enabler

Log in with your PIN, then enter the website code from your bmsupport.uk order or support ticket. The app knows what to do next: install your apps or connect your TV for staff help. Keep this computer and your TV on the same Wi-Fi.

Type your BM TV Enabler PIN to continue. Find it in Settings › BM TV Enabler PIN at bmsupport.uk (or your ticket/order Remote setup tab). It changes every 24 hours.

1. Enable TV debugging

Android / Google TV with Wireless debugging: pair with code

On the TV: Developer options › Wireless debugging › Pair device with pairing code. Type the code and the port after the colon on that screen. In TV address above, type the IP address & Port from the main Wireless debugging screen, e.g. 192.168.1.25:41235.

Untick any apps you don't want. Apps already on your TV are greyed out.

When the TV asks Allow USB debugging?, tick Always allow from this computer and choose Allow.

Code from bmsupport.uk

Type the code shown in Install Guides on the website. It holds your answers, so you won't be asked again here.

Do you need help from our staff?

Yes: we set up screen sharing and open a support ticket. Our staff then connect to your TV and install the apps for you (office hours only).

No: you choose and install the apps yourself.
